## Capacity analysis (stated cautiously)

The forward channel offers `T*n_symbols = 16` real uses. Under the per-round constraint `E‖x‖² ≤ 1` shared over 4 symbols, equal allocation gives power `1/4` per use, i.e. SNR = 1 (0 dB) and AWGN capacity

`C = 16 × 0.5 log2(1+1) = 8.0 bits`, below the `12`-bit message entropy.

Because feedback does **not** increase the capacity of a memoryless AWGN channel (it can improve the error exponent, not capacity), error-free transmission at `sigma^2=0.25` is **not achievable** by either system; a non-zero SER is expected. Fano's inequality gives a (loose) block-error floor `BLER ≥ 0.25`. We do **not** claim our SER is optimal — only that zero error is impossible in this regime. Capacity reaches the 12-bit message at `sigma^2 ≈ 0.137` (SNR ≈ 2.6 dB).
